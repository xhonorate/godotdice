# The in-run UI redo (Ledger)

October 24, 2026. The owner asked for a redo of everything the player looks at during a run: the style, the bottom dock, how a room asks for a stone or a die, tooltips, buffs and debuffs, health, block and Resonance, Void riders and phantom dice, the bag, and the creature plates. Three review rounds (a Design canvas, then two pages with pick buttons and notes) ended in the choices below. The review record lives in the artifact pages and in the mocks under `build/uishots/r2/` and `build/uishots/r3/` (`screen1a.html` is the final dock screen). This file records what was chosen and what was built.

## What was chosen

**The look: Ledger with facets, opaque.** Flat opaque plates (`#11141d`), a 3 px outline (`#2f3752`), a soft drop shadow (`#05070c` at about half strength, `DeepUi.drop_shadow`) falling 4 px down and a little right and feathered about 10 px, every corner cut straight across (about 12 px), bold Barlow. Pills and status chips are capsules with flat faceted ends, as the workshop's pills are; buttons are chamfered with the same soft shadow (on October 27 the owner found the old one-pixel-feathered hard shadow read as a solid black slab). Dice and socket rings stay round. The owner rejected frosted-glass and cut-crystal variants (`DeepUi.ledger`, `DeepUi.capsule`).

**The fight dock (layout 1a).** Left, a round portrait with health as a green ring and block as an outer blue arc, and one capsule under it reading `hp / max · shield N`, numbers once. Middle, the rail: six sockets and the Birthstone as big rings with names under them and nothing else; a socket the hand would fire wears a number badge top-right (damage, heal, ×mult) and a FIRES tag at its foot; Void riders fan out behind the socket's gem as dashed Void rings with a blue ×N badge top-left, and the hover plate lays them out in firing order. Resonance is the word and the number centred under the sockets: grey at nothing, in its own color by five, glowing and running through the rainbow past that, with sound-wave markers reaching out from either side of the word along the rail as it grows (the whole rail by about twenty-five), one wave per color of gem that rang, jolting and rippling at each gain, and climbing, quickening and burning past the end (the owner's revision of October 24, replacing the chain first chosen). Right, the five dice, large; phantoms fan behind the die they copy with a ×N badge; Reroll (rerolls left as pips) and Lock in sit centred under the dice. No headings, key hints or "this hand would" text. The dock never changes height.

**Statuses (form A).** One row of flat-ended capsule chips above the dock, over the room: green fill for help, red for harm, gold for run-long blessings, the effect's own mark and number inside, and under each chip a bar of segments for the turns it has left (a ghost bar where it has no clock, so every chip shares one baseline). The same chips stand on creature plates.

**Health and block on bars.** Block is drawn hatched past the end of health; an incoming blow hatches in red what it will take. The player's own reads off the ring.

**Tooltips.** A plate anchored to the hovered thing at 0 ms (in over ~90 ms, out over ~60 ms), gone when the pointer leaves, never pinned, never following the pointer. For a gem: `Name · COLOR · N ct · CUT`; WHEN IT FIRES as the trigger pictograph and the words for the stone's own cut only (the other cuts stay in the inspector); WHAT IT DOES with the gem sheet's keyword colors; a carat-multiplied number shown as its final value, underlined, with the working (base, carat, total on a gold plate) on hover; a THIS HAND strip lighting the tray dice that meet it and naming what lands; a footer `Right-click · everything`. One plate form for gems, riders, dice, phantoms, chips and creature moves (`view/ui/tips.gd`).

**The ask.** No bar between bag and dock. A block centred on the whole screen: the question, one big slot (two for the Hollow: gem and die), the chosen thing's name and carat on a capsule, Never mind and a confirm button, over a scrim that dims everything except the eligible stones and dice, which stay lit. A click on an eligible thing drops it into the slot at once; dragging onto the slot also works; the bag opens itself when it holds eligible stones. It serves the oddities, the Hollow, the well (a Stone slot and a Pyrite slider), the smithy and carver (a die slot), a trade with another player and a merchant's die trade-in. The Altar and the stall keep their in-room drops, with the ask as a fallback.

**The bag (C5 A).** A drawer with a reading pane (the hovered stone's card with Set, Sell and Look), Appraised and Raw as toggles (both on by default), Geodes behind a third toggle off by default, a sort row (Color, Carat, Grade) and a Fits filter; the sockets that take the hovered stone light green; raw stones show only their color and size class.

**Creature plates (M1), replaced October 28, 2026.** The plates over the creatures' heads covered too much of the room; eleven rounds of the plates rethink (artifact NMAGkwooypHHUKxsB4tR71, db `plates1`..`plates11`) replaced them with inset tags at the creatures' feet and a ledger at the side of the room, read with the creatures' action dice (docs/CREATURE_DICE.md). See "Creature tags and the ledger" below.

**Phone.** The same layout as the desktop for now; the thumb console and phone sheets in the review are reference only.

**Feedback (R1, approved as drawn).** Die states: idle, a 2 px lift on hover over 80 ms, a 5 px lift with a spring when chosen, 350 ms rerolling, locked, phantom. Socket states: empty, set, would-fire lit in the same frame, a jolt as it fires, an ember after, hatched when buried or clouded. Buttons: hover 100 ms, a squash to 96% over 60 ms on press, 40% when disabled, a halo while the party waits, 300 ms held-to-confirm. Rules: never late, the dock never moves, one spring curve (overshoot 1.08, settled by 180 ms), a sound per touch, the fewer-flashes setting respected.

## What was built (October 24, 2026)

**The kit.** `DeepUi.ledger` is the plate (opaque fill, outline, soft drop shadow: the engine's shadow is the box again, offset, solid inside and feathered `shadow_size` wide, so only opaque fills get one or the solid heart shows through), `DeepUi.capsule` the capsule with flat faceted ends (the workshop pills' shape, through `facet_ends`; the owner turned down pointed ends on October 24), `DeepUi.ledger_button` and `ledger_primary` the buttons. `project.godot` sets `gui/timers/tooltip_delay_sec` to 0 and the theme's TooltipPanel wears the plate, so every plain tooltip in the game is instant and Ledger. The kit's `Bar` has a `cap` mode: block hatched past the end of health, a counted blow hatched in red.

**Instant plates.** `view/ui/tips.gd` wires any control (`Tips.attach(control, build, opts)`): the plate is built on every hover, stands over the control (under it when there is no room), fades in over 90 ms and out over 60 ms, follows the control if it moves, and goes when the pointer leaves or anything is clicked. A plate opened with `keep` takes the pointer, so a number inside it can be hovered; `Tips.show_sub` opens the working beside it. `view/ui/plates.gd` builds the one plate form: `gem` (title line, the trigger at the stone's own cut, a rule, the words in keyword colors, the weight multiplier underlined with its working on hover, the riders in firing order, the THIS HAND strip with the dice that meet it lit and what lands; no headings and no helper text), `die` (faces with the one showing lit, what is cut into it, the phantoms behind it), `words` for chips and buttons.

**Status chips.** `EffectChips.Chip` is a capsule (green for help, red for harm, gold for a run-long blessing, violet for a creature's trait) with the effect's own mark and number inside, and a `TurnBar` under it: one segment a turn left for the statuses in `COUNTS_DOWN` (and a creature's charge, flee and every-nth countdowns), a ghost bar for everything else so every chip in a row shares one baseline. The same chips stand on the dock's row, the party's tabs and the creature plates.

**The fight dock** (`view/battle/battle_screen.gd`, `_build_page`). Left, `VitalsRing` (`view/battle/vitals_ring.gd`): the face in a round plate (`Roster.Portrait.round`), health as the ring with the kit's ghost and flash, a counted blow hatched in red, and one capsule under it with the health numbers. Block is its own number on a badge at the top of the ring (the owner's revision of October 24, after The Bazaar) and an arc outside the ring that grows both ways round from the top: a whole ring when block matches full health, and past that (`OVERFLOW`, up to three times health) the arc thickens and brightens and a breathing lattice of pale blue hexagons comes up over the face, rim first (`_draw_hexes`; steady under the fewer-flashes setting). Middle, the rail: sockets of `SOCKET_EDGE` (86) with the gem inside and its name under it, nothing else; a lit socket wears the figure it would land (`Plates.badge`) big and bold at its top-right corner, outlined so it reads over the stone (no FIRES tag, no pill); a buried or clouded socket is hatched (`SocketRing.blocked`). Void riders are rings fanned up and to the right behind the socket (`view/battle/fan.gd` sets the step), each with a faint blue-tinted copy of its gem inside (`VOID_GHOST`); the rail builds them in a zero-height `Riders` row before the card so they draw under it and take no room, with a `×N` count at the top-left corner that opens the gathering; past the third rider the count stands for the rest, so `_socket_cards` still has one control per flat rail index. Under the rail `ResonanceMeter` (`view/battle/resonance_meter.gd`) centres the mark, the word and the number (`box`, `number`: what the screen's flights and marks anchor to), grey to lavender over 0–5, rainbow-cycling with a glow past 5; its `_draw` (additive) lays a bundle of eighteen thin strands out from either side of the word to `value / 25` of the rail, each with its own wavelength, pace, height and faintness (four of them bright threads), colored along their length by the palette of what rang (`contributions`, set by `_sync_rail` from each firing gem's share of the forecast count, the colors blending into one another), pinched where they leave the word and where they end; motes of the same light drift off them (`CPUParticles2D`, off under the fewer-flashes setting); each gain is a jolt (`DeepUi.spring`) and a brightening ripple travelling outward; past 25 the strands climb, quicken and cycle the rainbow and the motes rise as flames. The mark, the word and the number share one color (the lavender ramp, then one slowly cycling pale hue past five) and the dock's bold face, the word spaced out a little. Both docks lay the same rows (`OVER_ROW`, `DIE_WORDS`, `BUTTONS_ROW`, `METER_ROW`; the run dock lays them empty): the rail column fills the dock's height, the rail stands level with the dice, and the meter takes what is left under the names and centres itself in it, so a gem or a die stands exactly where it stood as a fight begins and ends (checked pixel for pixel in `build/uiredo/cmp_battle_dock.png` and `cmp_landing_dock.png`). Right, the dice at `DIE_EDGE` (100), no key hints; a Refract's phantoms are `PhantomRing`s fanned up and to the right behind the die they copy, each with a faint copy of the die showing the face it carries, with a count; Reroll (the rerolls left as pips) and Lock in centred under them in the Ledger look. The one line over the dice carries Puck's Motley and a word of state; the instructional hints are gone. The run dock (`view/run/run_dock.gd`) is the same dock at rest.

**Creature tags and the ledger** (October 28, 2026; `view/battle/creature_tag.gd`, `enemy_panel.gd`, `creature_die_window.gd`, `creature_look.gd`). Each creature wears an inset tag over its head (`TAG_LIFT`; under its feet, `TAG_DROP`, until October 30, and still there for a creature too tall for it in the widest view): its action dice stand in at the left, real `DiceView` solids with their engraved marks (one die big and over the corner; several nearly as big, overlapped in a fan with the front one by the name and each behind it a step left and a little higher, `dice_spots`), then its name, its health with its block hatched over the end of the bar and what this hand would take hatched red, and one row of grey pills, a move's mark in its kind's ink and the one number it lands (`Look.number`; a Warden's pills also say their names while the row stays narrower than `NAMED_MOST`; nothing wraps); its block is a chip on the tag's corner, its statuses hang under it with their turns over each chip (the party's wear theirs under), in room always kept for a row of them (`CHIP_ROOM`), so a status landing never moves the tag, and a trait it pinned on itself until its next action (a Lantern, Static, Hush, Roller, Gravity, Paradox) is a violet chip with one turn on it. Tags close together are eased apart sideways, a step away from their creatures only where the row is full, never over the room's title, the dock or a party tab over it (`_spread_tags`, `_tag_floor`); the camera widens only until the highest tag fits under the title (a Warden's room opens 3-4 degrees for it) and holds still through the fight (October 30: widening it for tags at the feet ran away to its widest, since lifting the view drops the floor further down the screen). When a creature throws, a die from behind the stack first swaps round to the front over the others (`SWAP`, 0.22 s) and keeps that place after; its die grows 1.7 times about its right edge and middle, comes in front, tumbles and lands, and what it landed on is a pill over the die ("Rake 3×3 → you"; a per-reroll price says the rerolls and what they came to); the die goes back as the action ends. The ledger (`enemy_panel.gd`) stands at the top of the room on the side away from the creature it reads (`_ledger_right`), free to stand over the creatures and tags on its side down to the dock (`_ledger_room`; the owner, October 30), since pointing at one it covers sends it across: whose it is (your target, looking at, acting), its name and dice, its health and block, then a row a face move per die (a rule and the mark in its kind's ink, its name, its words with Marked, Poison, Burn, Curse, Dulled, Festering, Strength, Stunned, Drink, Acid Armour, Sturdy, Steadfast and Waterlogged underlined and explained on hover, and its share of the die as pips), the matches its faces make, the moves the fight calls or that wait for a face, and its traits; the face that has just landed is lit. Where its rows would stand it taller than the room keeps it reads one line a move, and past that a die at a time with a button a die. The pointer on a creature or its tag hands the ledger to it, and the ledger holds while the pointer is on it. Right-click a creature or its tag for the die window: the room dims, the die stands large and turnable beside the rows of what each of its faces does (pointing at a row turns that face to the front), a button a die for several, Esc or a click outside to close, and its foot opens the Bestiary page. A re-etched face, a grown die or head, an echo becoming something else (its model is made again) and a creature fleeing show on the tag and in the ledger as they land. On the dock, fog names the creature that breathed it, rubble for the fight says so, a gem a Star Leech clings to wears a ring of violet barbs and the leech's mark, and a die a Paradox Moth left blank is dimmed violet with BLANK over it. `tests/test_enemy_ui.gd` checks all of it.

**The ask** (`view/run/ask_sheet.gd`). Its scrim sits under the dock and the block over it, standing just above the dock and its drawer; the chosen thing's name and carat are centred in the capsule under its slot. `open(spec)` takes slots (kind, items, refusals, `most` for the acid bath's racks) and extras (a face of the chosen die, an inclusion of the chosen stone, a slider of pyrite). The run dock's `spotlight` lights the eligible things and steps the rest back, opens the bag when one is in it, and a plain click on a lit thing emits `picked`, which the ask takes; dragging onto a slot does the same. `DescentScreen._ask_spec` turns an oddity choice into a spec; `_page_oddity_work` opens the ask for every piece of work that wants one of the party's things (a pattern or a merchant's case stays a card), the landing's well goes through it, a trade offers a stone through it, and `RunDock.offer` (a merchant's die trade-in) opens it with the key `dock_offer`. `_settle_ask` closes it when the room no longer wants it.

**The bag** (`RunDock._build_drawer`). Appraised and Raw toggles on by default and Geodes off, a sort row (Color, Carat, Grade) and a Fits toggle, the tiles beside a reading pane that opens on the stone last pointed at (its picture, name and grade marks, its words, and Set into the first socket that takes it, Sell at a stall, Look); pointing at a stone also lights the sockets that would take it. A raw stone reads only its color and size class. Since November 5, 2026 it is a column at the right; see "The bag as a column" below.

**Consistency pass (October 25, 2026).** From a review of the run against the workshop and of a four-player fight, all built:
- Every raised panel (`DeepUi.raised`, so the choice cards, the bench, stone cards and the workshop's panels) and every button (`DeepUi.button_style`, `DeepUi.primary`) is in the Ledger look: a 2 px outline and a soft drop shadow, nearly opaque panels made fully opaque, no glow on the call to action. The close look and the pause menu have Ledger frames.
- The fight's dice rest at the thumbnails' turn (`DiceView.tilt`), so a die looks the same in a fight and on the dock outside it. Since Oct 26 that turn is slight (`DiceView.TILT`): the rolled face looks nearly straight out, so its number is the one that reads, with only a sliver of the sides showing (it was a three-quarter turn, and a 6 read "m6").
- Type: Jaro for places, Cinzel only for gold section headings, Barlow for what is read in play; creature names on their plates are Barlow bold capitals.
- In a fight the top strip leaves out the party's health (it is on the tabs and the dock); the room's corner title drops the depth (the strip has it) and names only an elite or a Warden's room.
- A creature's unrolled dice say their size ("d4") in grey instead of a yellow "?".
- Seat tabs are outlined in their seat's color (no stripe); YOU is a flat-ended capsule. Alone, the reader's tab is kept unseen, so their chips stand where they stand in a party. A delver's passive is not a chip: it is said in the plate over their face and in their tab's tooltip. Another player's page shows their state as button-sized Ledger plates where the reader's buttons are.

**Accessibility and feel (October 25, 2026).** Plates come up for touch and for keyboard and gamepad: a finger held on a thing for `Tips.LONG_PRESS` (0.42 s) on a touch screen shows its plate and lifting it puts the plate away, and a thing that takes the focus shows its plate (rail gems take the focus, as the dice do). A gem the hand leaves dark steps its stone back but only greys its name to MUTED, and empty sockets say "Any" in MUTED, both readable at the text floor. The ask fades in (scrim over 0.16 s) and out (block and scrim over 0.12 s), and the dock's things step back and forward on the same beat. Each rise in Resonance rings a soft chime a step higher, up to the end of the rail. Block gained jumps the shield badge and flares white-blue along the stretch of arc it added (the whole ring when the arc was already whole); half strength under the fewer-flashes setting.

**The bag as a column (November 5, 2026, playtest round 2).** The owner picked "a column on the right, opposite the chart": the bag is no longer a drawer over the dock but a column down the right edge of the screen (`RunDock._drawer`, named `Bag`), `COLUMN_WIDTH` (214) wide. Under it the Appraised, Raw and Geodes toggles with their counts and Fits; then the tiles, `GEM_TILE` (72, the run bench's size) two across, as many rows as the column's height holds; more stones than that are turned a page at a time with a pager at the column's foot (`turn_page`, `_pages["bag_page"]`), never scrolled. The whole column takes a set stone back and a stall's goods; the tiles drag onto sockets, the lens and the scales as before; the B key, the bag pill, the ask's spotlight and a stall drag open it as before, and `bag_anchor` (the pill on the strip) is still where stones fly to.

**The carver's face picker sized to the die (November 5, 2026).** The ask's face picker (`AskSheet._extras`) sizes its chips the way the run bench's dice tab does (`AskSheet.face_edge`: 30 px to a d8, 27 to a d12, 23 to a d20, 19 above), six across, so a d30 is five rows.
