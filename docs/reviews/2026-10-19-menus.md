# Menu and interaction review — October 19, 2026

**Owner decision:** All suggestions and mockups are rejected; do not implement them. Soundtrack access was intentionally absent, and the added entry has been reverted. The original audit results below describe the earlier pass.

The workshop and menus work through the tested mouse flows. Three small defects remain fixed: menu keyboard events were swallowed, repeated Delvers browsing got stuck, and Enter did nothing in the LAN address field. Exposing Soundtrack was an incorrect change and has been undone.

## Method and evidence

- After reverting Soundtrack access, the navigation suite passed **16 assertions, zero failures**, including the requirement that the entry stays absent. The rendered audit no longer attempts the removed flow; the counts below are from the original audit.
- Engine: Godot 4.7.2, macOS, Apple M1, Forward+/Metal for the rendered pass.
- `tools/review_menus.gd` drove a real window with mouse/key events: **74 checks initially, 72 on the post-fix repeat, zero interaction failures in either**. Counts vary with the daily Geode's available rarity pages. It used fresh and unlocked test profiles, visited the workshop and modal flows, turned inspector pages/models, and completed an animated appraisal. The latest machine-readable trace is `build/review-2026-10-19/menu-interaction.json`; console output is `menu-interaction.log` and `menu-interaction-after.log` beside it.
- `tests/test_menu_navigation.gd`: **18 assertions, zero failures** after fixes. Before fixes, the first regression pass failed seven navigation assertions; the subsequent Soundtrack/LAN regression pass failed two assertions. The final suite also checks focus wraps within the menu and game shortcuts remain blocked behind it. It has been added to `tools/run_checks.py`.
- The project's existing `test_screens.gd` and `test_fit.gd` provide wider scenario/layout coverage, including constructed full inventories, guest/host layouts, every skill/creature inspector, and in-run screens. Both were rerun after the menu fixes: **120** and **1,779** assertions respectively, with zero failures. These are automated scenario/layout checks, not observations of every possible human interaction.
- Save paths were isolated: `user://review_menus_20261019`, `user://test_menu_navigation`, and the existing suites' `user://test_scratch`. No real profile or run was used.

## Fixed

| Issue | Reproduction and impact | Change and verification |
|---|---|---|
| Menu rejected keyboard navigation | Open Esc menu. Resume has focus, but Tab, Enter and arrow keys do nothing. Settings also loses focus after rebuilding its page. | Let control navigation reach the focused menu control, retain the existing handler for gameplay keys, and focus the first control on Settings/Controls. | Delvers Q/E became stuck | Unlock Ardor, Vesper and Cadence. Open Delvers; E twice still shows Vesper. Verified repeated forward/back browsing, and that browsing dossiers leaves the equipped character unchanged. |
| LAN address ignored Enter | Open Play together, focus the LAN address, press Enter. Steam's ID field submits, but LAN did nothing. | Submit the LAN address on Enter using the same existing join signal as its button. Verified the address/port signal and that the key does not accidentally start an expedition. |

## Console findings

The initial rendered audit had no gameplay/UI script errors. Shutdown printed `2 shaders of type ParticlesShaderRD were never freed` and `2 ... MaterialStorage6ShaderE ... leaked at exit`, even after freeing the app and waiting for frames. A minimal Godot-only reproduction isolated the trigger: assigning a material-bearing draw mesh before assigning a `GPUParticles3D` node's process material. Reversing those assignments avoids the leaked shader. `BattleFx.ambient` now assigns the process material first; normal warm shader caches remain intact. `tests/test_view.gd` then passed **2,645 assertions** without its previous headless RID warning. The full rendered menu audit was repeated and exited cleanly with **72 checks**, no script errors and no shader/RID warnings. `tools/repro_particle_shader.gd` retains the two orders for comparison (`-- --mesh-first` reproduces the failure).

Steam initialization also reported that no Steam client/installation was found. That explains unavailable Steam coverage in this environment, but the noise is still visible on a normal solo startup and deserves a clearer availability state.

These results cover automated real-window interaction plus scenario/layout tests on this Mac. They do not establish accessibility compliance, comfort for a human with visual/motor impairments, Steam success, mobile/web input behavior, multiple monitor behavior, or usability at every text/window scale.
