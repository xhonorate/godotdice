# Skill gem catalogue and future work

Updated October 11, 2026 from [content/deep_cut.json](content/deep_cut.json). The current pack contains **104 skill gems: 90 standard gems and 14 Mythic Opals**, after the 38 October 2026 additions listed below their color tables (26 Common, 32 Uncommon, 27 Rare, 5 Legendary among the standard gems). Standard gems are tiered as a pyramid — **21 Common, 17 Uncommon, 13 Rare, 4 Legendary** (rebalanced October 6, 2026; previously 12 / 18 / 20 / 4). The six character Birthstones are separate. All requested gem revisions and the ten additions below are implemented.

| Color  | Role        | Skills |
| ------ | ----------- | -----: |
| Red    | Damage      |     11 |
| Blue   | Guard       |      9 |
| Green  | Sustain     |      8 |
| Violet | Control     |      9 |
| Gold   | Fortune     |     10 |
| White  | The hand    |      8 |
| Opal   | Every color |     11 |

Each slash-separated ladder runs **Poor / Fair / Good / Fine / Perfect**. Pair and triple thresholds refer to matched face value. “Allies” includes the caster; “weakest ally” means the living ally with the lowest HP percentage. Adjacent enemies are the nearest living neighbors in the displayed row, with the right neighbor preferred for a single adjacent hit.

Formulas are base values before ordinary carat/inclusion scaling. **Flawless also multiplies magnitude by 1.5 and base Resonance gain by 3.** Every gem effect answers to carat: amounts take the magnitude multiplier, and whole-number effects (statuses, rerolls, face upgrades, temporary rank upgrades, Void copies, Thrive’s +1 max HP) proc more often instead. Only prices/refunds stay exact, and result-based riders, which read a result that already scaled. Ember, Crosscut, Tempo, Miasma, Shelter, Apex, Jackpot and Wager carry a Flawless multiplier that applies only under the line’s condition and is shown as its own step in the gem’s count. Keyword definitions live in hover tooltips and [Buffs.md](Buffs.md).

New rules that came with them: Spikes last the fight for everyone; players can carry Strength; Hone and Hardening count their fires for the run (`run_counts`, kept by the run), and a Flawless Hone also its kills, a Flawless Hardening the times its owner's Block was broken, by anything; Chainmail, Contagion and Rebound last the turn (a Flawless Contagion the fight); Arsenic's Envenom lasts the fight; Bloodletting's blood is a price (`hp_cost`) that never takes the last point; a Placer's stones join the fight's spoils on a win; Hydrophane's drops are kept on the die. Invert (approved October 24, 2026, the Reverse Image idea under its new name) reads what the gem in the socket before it really did on its last go this turn (`gem_results`, kept per stone and reset each turn) and gives a share of it back turned around; its share grows by carat steps, and the "inverted" keyword hover says what turns into what. The creature moves that held the new names were renamed: the Glazier's Anneal, the Anvil Knight's Quench and the Drowned Choir's Requiem.

## Implementation notes

- **Lifeline:** stacks on each living ally. The next lethal direct hit, Poison tick, or retaliation consumes every stack and restores that much HP (up to maximum). Flawless applications also store that amount of revival Block. It does not revive an already downed ally and has no once-per-fight activation limit.
- **Permanent means this run:** Glimmer changes physical die faces and Thrive raises max HP by 1 per proc of its Flawless line (so more on a heavier stone). Both survive later fights without altering the stored collection, and so do the Carat a Flawless Enrich gives its neighbours and the Carat a Flawless Black Opal gains for each gem it absorbs. Glimmer also updates the current roll; phantom dice cannot gain permanent faces.
- **Fight-only gem growth:** Polish grants adjacent Clarity, Facet adjacent Cut, and Enrich adjacent Carats. Flawless Polish/Facet stay adjacent, and a neighbour already at Flawless/Perfect gains 1 Carat instead. A Flawless Enrich gives its neighbours exactly 1 Carat for the rest of the run, however heavy the stone, and is destroyed; a Void copy only gives a fight’s carat and just goes, and a Doublet wearing it gives the same and stays. Otherwise each proc grants another point, so heavier stones grant more. Bonuses stack and follow the gem within the fight; Cut/Clarity stop at their normal highest tier. Temporary Clarity enables its Resonance, magnitude and Flawless bonuses while preserving existing inclusions. Stored gems are unchanged.
- **Pyrite:** Wager/Stake may spend bag Pyrite plus combat earnings. Each activation and replay pays separately; insufficient funds fizzle even with Star/forced activation. Wager refunds 2× the price only on a kill; a Flawless Wager holding twice the price deals double and refunds 4×. Stake gives +50%, multiplied by its carat steps; a Flawless Stake stakes the whole bank instead, up to 100 Pyrite times its carat curve (1,000 at 24 carats), at a flat 1% per Pyrite (owner's ruling, November 7, 2026). Prices/refunds are never multiplied by carats or elite/warden rewards. On defeat, unbanked earnings/refund profit are lost; net spending still reduces the bag balance, to a minimum of zero.
- **Result-based riders:** Riposte reads actual HP damage, Shatter actual Block removed (a Flawless Shatter’s owner gains that Block), Tithe actual Pyrite gained, and Bulwark actual Block gained. These riders do not receive a second magnitude multiplier. Mortar grants its percentage of current Block, scaled by magnitude, as Retain at activation; Retain has no cap. As its owner’s next turn opens, a Flawless Mortar deals damage equal to what their Retain will keep, read after the creatures have had their turn.
- **Curse:** one base stack per rolled 1; 0/1/2/3/4 max HP removed per rolled 1. Curse and max HP loss are separate Ward-blockable applications. Max HP cannot fall below 1. Max HP loss is scaled by magnitude. Flawless then attacks for current Curse stacks × magnitude, subject to ordinary target damage modifiers. The status remains 10% per stack, capped at 10.
- **Jackpot / Lucky Seven:** Jackpot’s 5× bonus requires Flawless and at least five matched dice, and is shown as its own step in the count. Lucky Seven with Flawless and at least three 7s resolves seven hits plus seven payouts in total, even with four or more 7s. Ordinary magnitude scaling still applies to each reward.
- **Apex:** uses the highest qualifying individual die once, with thresholds 20/19/18/17/16. Its Flawless doubling requires that selected die to be a crown (showing its own top face), and is shown as its own step in the count.
- **Tailings:** pays for each socket in this turn's fizzle list that it has not already collected, never counting its own socket. Each Tailings stone (a Doublet wearing it counts as its own) remembers how far down the list it has been paid, so replays, Prelude extra goes and fires-twice repeats find nothing new and fizzle, while a gem that fizzles after Tailings fired is paid on its next go. A socket that fizzles twice in one turn is listed once. The paid record clears at rail begin. The base payout scales with carats. With 5 or more gems dark this turn, a Flawless Tailings throws every die again and restarts the rail, its dark count at nothing and its Resonance kept; a rail that keeps coming back is stopped by the rail’s play cap.
- **Appraise:** identifies the first raw bag stone (up to two when Flawless), reveals its inclusions and deals its current Pyrite sale value × magnitude as one hit. The stone count is exact. Already appraised stones cannot pay again. Appraisal persists to the run; forecasts do not mutate real inventory.
- **Chosen defaults:** Bloom’s Charged bonus is party-wide. Gilded Armor grants Block worth 10/15/20/25/30% of available Pyrite, and when Flawless every blow its Block stops this turn pays 2 Pyrite, scaled by carat; Appraise requires a pair; Enrich requires distinct values and grants its neighbours +1 Carat. Refract adds one phantom per normal proc, independent of remaining rail length; Flawless, the phantom is a size bigger than the largest die, and a further proc starts from the phantom.
- **Marked (October 23, 2026):** spent by the whole gem, Birthstone tier set or creature move that lands on the bearer, not by its first hit: every blow of a Flurry, a Barrage, a Legion or a Thousand Cuts takes the bonus, and the marks come off once that gem is done. Spikes, reflected blows and ticks never spend them, and a Flawless Etch’s Marks are never spent at all.
